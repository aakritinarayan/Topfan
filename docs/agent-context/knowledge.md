# TopFan creator platform

Navigation (left sidebar): NEW POST, LIVESTREAM, Home, Feed, Activity, Messages, Monetize, Wallet, Insights & Fans, Resources. Avatar menu (top right): Edit Preferences, Terms & Privacy Policy, Help Center & FAQ, Sign Out. Preferences has 16 tabs: Profile, Welcome Message, Interests, Creator Type, Customer Support, Notifications & Newsletters, NFT Wallet, Monetization, Privacy, Password & Integrations, White Label Site, Outbound Emails, Link In Bio, Delegation, Topic Tags, Groups, Gamification.

## Features (official name, where, what it does)
- New Post (Sidebar › NEW POST): Publish a video, images, article, audio, reel, poll, giveaway, link, fundraiser or text post. Posts can be locked for subscribers or a price.
- Livestream (Sidebar › LIVESTREAM): Start a livestream now, or schedule one as an event in Monetize › Events & Ticketing.
- Feed (Sidebar › Feed): Your public profile and posts, as fans see them.
- Home (Sidebar › Home): Your public Home page (the White Label Site landing page).
- Subscriptions (Monetize › Subscriptions): Monthly plans fans subscribe to (Gold, Silver, Bronze). Each plan has prices for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, optional discounts and free trials, and benefits.
- Newsletters (Monetize › Newsletters): Write and send newsletters to fans, optionally paid. Sending uses your Outbound Emails sender domain.
- Experiences (Monetize › Experiences): Sell personal experiences such as shoutouts, video calls and 1:1 sessions. Fans' orders appear in Wallet › My Fulfillment Requests.
- Courses (Monetize › Courses): Sell a course with lessons.
- Downloadables (Monetize › Downloadables): Sell digital files fans can download.
- Merchandise (Monetize › Merchandise): Sell merchandise through Shopify or an external link. Physical orders appear in fulfillment.
- NFTs (Monetize › NFTs): List external NFTs. (Your MetaMask connection is in Preferences › NFT Wallet.)
- Events & Ticketing (Monetize › Events & Ticketing): Create events and sell tickets (native tickets or Bandsintown import). Events appear on your public event listing; livestreams can be scheduled here.
- VIP Offers (Monetize › VIP Offers): Special offers and deals for fans.
- Portfolios (Monetize › Portfolios): Group videos into portfolios and series fans can watch (public Videos pages).
- Discount Codes (Monetize › Discount Codes): Create discount codes for your plans and products.
- Advertisements (Monetize › Advertisements): Review brands that want to advertise with you and approve or decline them.
- Activity (Sidebar › Activity): Recent likes, comments, purchases and follows. (Notification settings are separate, in Preferences.)
- Messages (Sidebar › Messages): Priority, General and Message Lists inboxes; message fans, paid DMs and automated messages.
- Automated Messages (Messages › Automated Messages): Messages sent automatically, for example when a fan subscribes.
- Insights & Fans (Sidebar › Insights & Fans): Fan list, Fan Relationship Management, segmentation, Earning Metrics and Engagement Metrics.
- Fan Relationship Management (Insights & Fans › Fan Relationship Management): Your fan list: top supporters, most engaged, top recruiters, and (once you have Groups) group membership.
- Engagement Metrics (Insights & Fans › Engagement Metrics): How fans engage with your posts.
- Segmentation & Outreach (Insights & Fans › Segmentation & Outreach): Filter fans by criteria and reach out to that segment.
- Earning Metrics (Insights & Fans › Earning Metrics): Revenue over time by stream.
- Current Balance (Wallet › Current Balance): Available balance, withdrawals, payout account and saved cards.
- Transactions (Wallet › Transactions): Every payment in and out.
- Referrals (Wallet › Referrals): Referral earnings (referrals also appear in Link in Bio and Advertisements).
- Loyalty History (Wallet › Loyalty History): Points history from gamification.
- My Purchases (Wallet › My Purchases): What you've bought on TopFan.
- Refund Requests (Wallet › Refund Requests): Review fans' refund requests and approve or decline each one.
- My Fulfillment Requests (Wallet › My Fulfillment Requests): Experience and merchandise orders waiting for you to fulfill.
- Profile (Preferences › Profile): Name, username, picture, bio and profile details.
- Welcome Message (Preferences › Welcome Message): The message (and video) new fans see when they join.
- Interests (Preferences › Interests): Interests used for discovery.
- Creator Type (Preferences › Creator Type): Your creator category.
- Customer Support (Preferences › Customer Support): How YOUR fans reach your support. (TopFan's own help is Resources › Creator Support and Help Center & FAQ.)
- Notifications & Newsletters (Preferences › Notifications & Newsletters): Which notifications you receive, and newsletter recipient settings. (The Activity page is separate.)
- NFT Wallet (Preferences › NFT Wallet): Your connected MetaMask wallet for NFTs. Not where your earnings are (that's Wallet).
- Monetization (Preferences › Monetization): Settings for tips, paid DMs and subscriber-only chat and comments. (Your products and plans are in Monetize.)
- Privacy, Password & Integrations (Preferences › Privacy, Password & Integrations): Privacy, password, sign-in methods, Google Calendar, blocked users, and pause or delete your account.
- White Label Site (Preferences › White Label Site): Your own website on your domain: domain and DNS, logo, colors, SEO and the cover page.
- Cover page editor (Preferences › White Label Site › Cover page): Edit the cover (landing) page of your White Label Site.
- Outbound Emails (Preferences › Outbound Emails): Send newsletters and emails from your own domain. Needs DNS records added at your domain provider, then verification.
- Link In Bio (Preferences › Link In Bio): A separate public links page with its own editor (not your White Label Site).
- Delegation (Preferences › Delegation): Give team members Content or Financial, Content access, with revenue share per stream.
- Topic Tags (Preferences › Topic Tags): Tags fans use to filter your feed. Topic Tags show on the feed once at least 2 are active.
- Groups (Preferences › Groups): Micro-communities: create a group, assign fans in Insights & Fans, then restrict posts, products, offers, chat or DMs to it.
- Gamification (Preferences › Gamification): Points fans earn, points they can buy, badges, and the leaderboard on your public profile.
- Announcements (Resources › Announcements): News from TopFan.
- Guides (Resources › Guides): Tips and tutorials for using TopFan.
- Service Providers (Resources › Service Providers): Service providers who can help with your business.
- Creator Support (Resources › Creator Support): Contact TopFan's creator support team.
- Help Center & FAQ (Avatar menu › Help Center & FAQ): Frequently asked questions.
- Store (Public profile › Store): Your public store. The order of items is set from the Store button on your profile, not in Monetize.
- Competitions (Public Home › Competitions): Your public list of events and competitions.
- Videos (Public Home › Videos): Your public videos and series.
- Creator Quest (White Label setup › Provisioning (mini-games)): Optional mini-games on the provisioning screen while your site is set up. Not the same as platform Gamification (fan points and badges).

## How features connect
- Groups: create in Preferences › Groups → assign fans in Insights & Fans (Fan Relationship Management) → restrict posts, products, offers, portfolios, chat or DMs to the group.
- Topic Tags: create in Preferences › Topic Tags → at least 2 must be active before they show → use them on posts → fans filter the feed. The Feed's gear icon links to Topic Tags.
- Gamification: configure in Preferences › Gamification → leaderboard on the public profile → points appear in Wallet › Loyalty History and Earning Metrics.
- Paid DMs and tips: set in Preferences › Monetization → used in Messages and posts → earnings appear in Wallet and Insights.
- Newsletters: create in Monetize › Newsletters → recipient settings in Preferences › Notifications & Newsletters → sending from your own domain needs Preferences › Outbound Emails verified → results appear in the Newsletters table.
- Events: create in Monetize › Events & Ticketing → public listing and ticket page → livestreams are scheduled as events → Google Calendar link is in Preferences › Privacy, Password & Integrations.
- White Label Site: domain and appearance in Preferences › White Label Site → cover page editor → public Home. Link in Bio is a separate public page with its own editor.
- Store order: set from the Store button on the public profile, not in Monetize.
- Integrations are spread out: Shopify (Monetize › Merchandise), Bandsintown (Events), MetaMask (Preferences › NFT Wallet), Google Calendar and sign-in methods (Preferences › Privacy), DNS (White Label Site and Outbound Emails), Google Tag Manager (White Label Site).
- Referrals appear in three places: Wallet › Referrals, Link in Bio, and Advertisements.
- Outbound Emails: TopFan shows DNS records (for example a verification TXT record and DKIM CNAME records) to add at the creator's domain provider; then the creator clicks verify. If the records aren't found, both steps show Failed. Changing DNS happens at the provider, never in TopFan or the assistant.
- Delegation: delegates get "Content" or "Financial, Content" access, with a revenue share per stream. (Exactly which screens each access opens is inferred in this demo.)
- Refund Requests: the creator approves or declines each request; nothing is refunded automatically.
- Withdrawals need a connected payout account. In this demo no money moves.

## Similar names (ask which one if unclear)
- Current Balance vs NFT Wallet: Do you mean your earnings and withdrawals, or your connected NFT wallet?
- Subscriptions vs Monetization: Do you mean what you sell (plans and products), or your tips and paid-DM settings?
- Creator Support vs Help Center & FAQ vs Customer Support: Do you want help from TopFan, or to set how your fans contact you?
- Activity vs Notifications & Newsletters: Do you want to see recent activity, or change which notifications you get?
- Groups vs Segmentation & Outreach: Do you want a lasting group (for restricted content), or a one-off filter of fans to reach out to?
- White Label Site vs Link In Bio: Do you mean your website (White Label Site), or your Link in Bio page?
- Gamification vs Creator Quest: Do you mean fan points and badges, or the Creator Quest mini-games?
